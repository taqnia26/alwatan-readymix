ﻣﻦ ﻧﺤﻦWho are we
Al-Watan Concrete Company is one of the leading national companies specializing in the production and
supply of ready-mix concrete. Headquartered in Riyadh, the company operates through a strategic
presence across several key locations within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
Since its establishment, Al-Watan Concrete Company has been driven by a clear vision to deliver reliable,
high-quality concrete solutions that meet the demands of residential, commercial, and infrastructure
projects, while adhering to the highest standards of quality and safety.
Al-Watan Concrete Company operates as part of an integrated business group comprising several
specialized companies and institutions, including:
Ready-mix concrete factories in Riyadh and Mulham
Companies specialized in the manufacturing and supply of raw materials
Logistics and transportation entities supporting efficient cement and material delivery
This integrated structure enables full control over the supply chain, ensuring continuity, efficiency, and
consistent quality across all projects.
Al-Watan Concrete Company strives to be a trusted partner in the Kingdom’s urban development journey
by delivering products and services that enhance project execution efficiency and support the objectives of
Saudi Vision 2030.
